# Name and logo

"FreakyMate" and the FreakyMate logo (`assets/logo/`) identify this project and its official releases by
Frank Roth. The GPL gives you rights to the code; it does not give you rights to the name and logo
(GPL-3.0 section 7(e)). This page says what is fine without asking and what needs my permission.

## Fine without asking

- Referring to the project truthfully: "runs FreakyMate", "based on FreakyMate", "works with FreakyMate".
- Sharing, installing and writing about unmodified official releases under their name, including screenshots and
  the logo in articles, videos and guides.
- Using the name in a fork's description to say where it comes from, for example "fork of FreakyMate".

## Needs my permission

- Using "FreakyMate", the logo or a confusingly similar name for a modified version or a fork. Please give your fork
  its own name and icon.
- Using the name or logo for a product, a printer, a display, a kit or a service, or in its advertising.
- Anything that suggests I endorse, support or certify your product.
- Domain names, app store entries or social media accounts named after FreakyMate.

Ask at **developer@freakydu.de**; for products see also [COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md).
