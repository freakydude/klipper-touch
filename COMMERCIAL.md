# Commercial use and licensing

FreakyMate is free software under the [GNU General Public License v3.0 or later](LICENSE). This page explains in
plain words what that means for companies and how to reach me if the GPL does not fit your plans. It is a summary,
not legal advice; the license text is what counts.

## What the GPL already allows

- **Using FreakyMate is free for everyone**, companies included: on your own printers, in a print farm, a workshop,
  a school or a lab. No license fee, no need to ask.
- **Changing and forking it** is allowed, as long as the result stays under the GPL.
- **Shipping it to others**, for example preinstalled on printers, displays or kits you sell, or as a download for
  your products, is allowed under the GPL's conditions: you provide the complete corresponding source code of
  FreakyMate including your changes under GPL-3.0-or-later, keep the license and copyright notices (including the
  third-party notices in `THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.html` of each package) and add no further restrictions.

## Dual licensing: talk to me

I hold the copyright of FreakyMate, and contributions from others will come in under a
[Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md), so I can also offer FreakyMate under other terms than the GPL.

If you want to build FreakyMate into a product and the GPL conditions do not fit, for example because your changes
or your firmware must stay closed, or if you are simply unsure whether your plans are covered: please get in touch.
I am open to offering a commercial license. I have not worked out terms and prices yet, so we would find them
together for your case.

Contact: **developer@freakydu.de**, subject "FreakyMate commercial license".

Two things a commercial license for FreakyMate does not cover:

- **Slint**, the UI toolkit FreakyMate is built with. FreakyMate uses Slint under its GPLv3 option; outside the GPL
  you need your own Slint license from [slint.dev](https://slint.dev). On embedded devices such as a display at a
  printer, Slint's royalty-free license does not apply.
- **The name and logo.** See [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md); using them for your product needs my permission,
  under the GPL as well as under a commercial license.
